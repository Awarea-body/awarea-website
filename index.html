Your nervous system turns down signals that stay the same. Low, steady tension fades from awareness, so you feel the ache at six o’clock, not the hours of build-up before it.

FAQ

Questions, answered plainly.

Anything else? Write to hello@awareabody.com.

Is this meditation?

Not quite. There’s no need to clear your mind or sit a certain way. Each session points your attention at specific sensations in one area, like where your weight is resting or whether your jaw is set, so you get quicker at noticing them on an ordinary workday.

Do I need to move?

No. Listen wherever you are, eyes open if you like, in whatever position you’re already in. Awarea is about noticing, not exercise.

Do I need an account?

Not for your first session. From Session 2 you’ll create a free account, so your sessions and progress are kept.

What if I take a break?

Nothing resets. Your next session waits for you. There are no streaks to lose and no scores to keep up.

What do you mean by noticing?

Paying attention to what’s happening inside your body right now: where you’re holding tension, how you’re breathing, where your weight is resting. You don’t try to change anything, you just register it. This sense is called interoception, and each session practises it in one area, so you start catching these signals on your own during the day.

How long until it becomes a habit?

On average, habits take around two months of daily repetition to feel automatic (Lally et al., 2010). The 21-session Foundation Block lays the groundwork, and more blocks open after it.
