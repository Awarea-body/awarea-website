Why this matters

You're not imagining it.

Two things the research is clear on.

Most fatigue can't even explain itself.

Unexplained fatigue is 2.7× more common than fatigue with a clear cause. Right now, 20.4% of adults are dealing with it.

The good news? This is trainable.

Interoception — noticing what's happening inside your body — sharpens with practice, like any other sense.

Frontiers in Public Health, 2023 — meta-analysis of 91 studies, 600,000+ participants Craig (2002); Garfinkel & Critchley (2013)
